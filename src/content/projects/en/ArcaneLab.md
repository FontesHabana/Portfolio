ArcaneLab is a scientific analysis platform designed to simplify the study of Ordinary Differential Equations (ODE). Developed with Python and Streamlit, the tool allows students to model and visualize dynamic systems intuitively and efficiently.

Beyond graphical representation, the application integrates critical features for numerical error analysis in computational solutions, providing a rigorous environment for the validation and study of advanced calculus.
