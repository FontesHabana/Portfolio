MuMind is a digital adaptation of the board game "Herd Mentality", designed to bring social dynamics into a mobile environment. Built with React and Tailwind CSS, the application stands out for its fluid interface and fully responsive architecture.

The project was awarded as a winner in GitHub's "For the Love of Code" Hackathon, recognizing not only its visual appeal but also its ability to deliver an intuitive, high-performance user experience on any device.
